Guides

Quick answers and printable guides for every screen in the system.

🧱 Outlier Diagnostics

Sounds every indicator across five detection layers (raw, rate, history, volume, peers) and classifies the result into 8 action types.

Use: pick the month, scan colored badges, press Why on any row for the five-layer breakdown and the full audit trail.

⚠️ Outlier Scan

Z-score statistical scan of the current month against historical windows.

Use: quick sweep of the most unusual values per rate; drill into peers popover.

✅ Quality Reports

Composite quality score (rule compliance + completeness + consistency, minus outlier penalty).

Use: monthly scorecard review per hospital, trends over time untouched by diagnostics.

🔔 Alerts

Failed validation rules per hospital and month.

Use: data-hygiene follow-up list; rule chips open the rule's own Why popup.

📝 Audit Log

Full verification of every derived number: calculation steps, traceability chain, data-auditor sweep.

Use: the single source of truth reached from any Why popup's audit button.

🔥 Root Cause

Explains WHY a hospital's score changed: component contributions + peer comparisons.

Use: monthly management review pre-read.

🔍 FAQ

What is the difference between Raw value and Rate? Between the 8 classifications? How are peers chosen?

Answered in the printable legend (top button) and inside each Why popup.

Every screen also has its own Explain/Why button — the fastest way to understand a number in context.
